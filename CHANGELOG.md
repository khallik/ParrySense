\## 0.1.0



Initial public release.



\### Added



\- Post-impact parry timing feedback.

\- `TOO EARLY`, `PARRY`, and `TOO LATE` classifications.

\- Signed millisecond timing display.

\- Visual representation of Valheim's 250 ms timed-block window.

\- Configurable early and late detection limits.

\- Configurable HUD position and appearance.

\- English and French HUD localization.

\- F8 toggle with on-screen status notification.



\### Design



\- Client-side only.

\- No server installation required.

\- No changes to combat mechanics.

\- No modification of the parry window.

\- No attack prediction or automatic blocking.
